PRACTICAL CYBER SAFETY
GUIDE · EDUCATION

Responding to online blackmail safely.

Threats and blackmail can be frightening. You deserve support. Focus on immediate safety, reducing further exposure, preserving essential details, and contacting appropriate services.

Immediate danger or risk of physical harm: contact local emergency help or a trusted person who can be with you. Do not wait for a response from a website contact inbox.

1. Pause before responding

Do not send more money, intimate material, passwords, or verification codes. Paying or complying does not guarantee the threats will stop. Avoid confrontations that could increase danger.

2. Keep a careful record

Where safe, save messages, account names, URLs, dates, payment instructions, and platform report numbers. Keep copies securely. Do not repost, forward, or send intimate images to people who offer to help. For minors or non-consensual intimate content, avoid downloading or circulating the material; seek appropriate official or specialist help.

3. Report the account or content

Use the platform's built-in reporting process and ask for support from a trusted person. If you are a woman in Bangladesh facing cybercrime, Bangladesh Police's official Police Cyber Support for Women (PCSW) page describes its service and provides contact information. Other cases should be directed to the appropriate official reporting or law-enforcement channel.

See official reporting resources ↗

4. Secure your accounts

Change passwords from a trusted device, enable multi-factor authentication, revoke suspicious sessions, and review recovery options. Tighten privacy settings and warn close contacts that impersonation messages may appear.

5. Get human support

Consider a trusted friend, family member, qualified support organization, or appropriate official service. You do not have to handle threats alone. If a device or account may be monitored, think through safety before changing settings or confronting the person.

What TACIB can and cannot do

TACIB's website provides general safety education and signposting. We are not law enforcement, a crisis line, a legal service, or an emergency takedown provider. We cannot promise anonymity, investigation, account recovery, or removal of content.

This guide is general information and not legal, medical, or crisis advice. Preserve your safety first.
