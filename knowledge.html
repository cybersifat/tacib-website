LEARN SAFELY

Practice in a legal lab.

Security tools are powerful. Use training environments, intentionally vulnerable virtual machines, and platforms that explicitly authorize testing. Do not try techniques against a school, employer, ISP, public website, or another person's device without explicit permission.

If you discover a possible weakness in a real service, stop at the minimum necessary evidence and follow the provider's responsible disclosure process. Do not publish private data or exploit instructions that could put users at risk.
