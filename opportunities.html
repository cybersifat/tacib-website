CAREERS & COLLABORATION

Build trust through useful work.

TACIB aims to build a mission-driven team over time. This page is for future role announcements and clearly scoped collaborations—not a claim that vacancies or funded roles are currently open.

OUR APPROACH TO PEOPLE

Clear roles.
Honest expectations.

Potential contributors may have interests in defensive security, cyber education, responsible research, writing, or data analysis. Work must be scoped around actual capability, supervision, privacy, and available resources.

TACIB will publish confirmed roles here when they are approved. An expression of interest does not create an employment relationship or promise future paid work.

Where a voluntary contribution is offered, the expected tasks, duration, access, credit, ownership of work, and unpaid nature should be agreed in advance. Unpaid contributions must never be presented as guaranteed future employment.

POTENTIAL WORKING AREAS

Where skills can contribute.

These are areas TACIB may develop. They are not current vacancies.

01 / SECURITY

Authorized security research

Lab-based practice, validation, documentation, and clearly scoped defensive testing.

02 / RESEARCH

Cyber research & writing

Source verification, threat-awareness explainers, and responsible editorial practice.

03 / EDUCATION

Community learning

Beginner-friendly tutorials, workshop design, translation, and digital-safety materials.

04 / DATA

Analysis & visualization

Careful analysis of suitable datasets with documented limitations and privacy safeguards.

What to include in an inquiry

  • A brief introduction and area of interest.
  • Links to public work samples or a portfolio, if available.
  • Realistic availability and what type of collaboration you are seeking.
  • No private credentials, sensitive personal records, or confidential work from another organization.

Please do not send unsolicited sensitive documents. TACIB may not be able to respond to every inquiry.
