# TACIB Website Strategy & Sitemap

## Positioning
TACIB remains **The Agency of Cyber Intelligence Bangladesh**, presented as an **independent private cyber intelligence initiative**. The public website should make clear that it is not a government agency, police unit, regulator, emergency service, or authority with investigative powers. The business side should fund a broader public-safety mission, not make unverified claims about size, access, credentials, or outcomes.

## Recommended navigation
- **Home** — mission, practical work, authorization-first process, public resources.
- **About** — purpose, values, institutional boundaries, and working streams.
- **Services** — service descriptions, scope, what is and is not offered.
- **Research** — methodology, evidence standards, official advisories, and future TACIB briefs.
- **Learn** — guides and awareness resources.
- **Get Help** — general next steps and official Bangladesh reporting routes.
- **Contact** — inquiry builder that opens the visitor's email client.
- Footer-only: responsible disclosure, privacy notice, terms, careers & collaboration.

## Services to advertise carefully
1. **Website security and exposure review:** scoped review of assets that the requester owns or is authorized to manage; specific deliverables and limitations agreed before work starts.
2. **Authorized vulnerability assessment / penetration testing:** only after written authorization, a named scope, test window, exclusions, stop conditions, and report recipients are agreed. Accept only targets and complexity TACIB can safely support.
3. **Website, email, and account hardening guidance:** foundational checklist and configuration guidance, not continuous monitoring or a guarantee of security.
4. **Public-information exposure review (OSINT):** public sources, a legitimate organizational scope, minimal collection, and no doxxing, stalking, private-account access, or personal targeting.
5. **Cybersecurity awareness and workshops:** phishing/scam recognition, MFA/password habits, privacy, and safe lab-based learning; dates and availability confirmed case by case.
6. **Digital safety navigation:** general guidance and signposting to platforms or official services. Do not promise account recovery, takedowns, criminal investigations, or legal outcomes.

## Do not advertise as currently available without verified capability and a safe operating process
- 24/7 SOC or managed security monitoring
- Forensic investigations or incident-response retainers
- Malware removal/cleanup as a guaranteed service
- Emergency non-consensual image/content takedowns
- Guaranteed account recovery or reputation removal
- Unqualified promises to investigate crimes or identify anonymous people
- Generic predictive modelling / enterprise BI unrelated to TACIB's core mission

Those may be considered later with specialist staff, tooling, insurance/contracting, documented processes, and trusted referral partners.

## Workstream names from the previous site
- **The Shadows of Secret:** ethical security learning, lab research, authorized defensive assessments.
- **Truth Lens:** cyber reporting, public-source analysis, awareness content.
- **Data Analysis Wing (DAW):** data-supported research and visual explanations.

The site describes these as **working streams in development**, not separate staffed departments. This preserves the brand history without implying an active team where one may not exist.

## Included launch features
- Responsive layout and mobile navigation
- Reusable dark navy/cyan identity and simple local SVG mark
- Accessible structure, focus states, skip link, and reduced-motion support
- Per-page titles and descriptions, Open Graph metadata, `robots.txt`, and `sitemap.xml`
- Local guide search (no search service or tracking)
- Email inquiry builder that opens the visitor's own email application and does not store submitted information on the site
- Official Bangladesh help/reporting links
- Responsible disclosure, privacy, terms, and collaboration pages
- Clear scope warnings and no fake testimonials, fake client logos, made-up metrics, or fake live threat feed

## Phase 2 — wait until the operating process is ready
- Professionally reviewed Bangla translations and a true language switch
- Regular TACIB-authored research briefs with evidence, dates, corrections, and defined confidence labels
- A real intake backend only after privacy, retention, spam handling, and secure transfer have been decided
- Client portal, booking, paid checkout, newsletter, or ticketing only if there is a real workflow and owner
- Service-specific detailed case studies only when verifiable, permissioned examples exist

## Launch blockers to verify
- Confirm the public contact inbox is active; currently `site-config.js` uses the email listed by the previous public TACIB site.
- Review all service claims against actual delivery capability.
- Review the privacy and terms drafts against real practices and applicable requirements.
- Confirm both `tacib.net` and `www.tacib.net` routes, TLS, and one canonical redirect.
- Preserve existing MX/SPF/DKIM/DMARC DNS records if domain email is configured.
- Keep the old Jimdo site available until useful URLs have been mapped or redirected where possible.
