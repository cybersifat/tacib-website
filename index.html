01 / WHY TACIB EXISTS

The internet should work for people.

Digital harm can affect a person's safety, privacy, livelihood, and trust. Good security is not only about tools. It is about people, evidence, and responsible action.

TACIB is an independent private initiative that aims to make cyber risks easier to understand and address. We bring together scoped defensive security work, public-source research, and accessible learning.

03 / HOW ENGAGEMENTS WORK

Clear from the start.

No mystery around what a service includes. Each engagement starts with a defined need and ends with clear next steps.

STEP 01

Understand

Describe the concern, asset, or learning goal without sending passwords or sensitive material.

STEP 02

Scope

Confirm ownership or authorization, boundaries, deliverables, timing, and fit.

STEP 03

Work responsibly

Perform only agreed tasks and avoid unnecessary data access or disruption.

STEP 04

Explain findings

Share actionable outcomes and limitations honestly, including when a request is outside scope.
