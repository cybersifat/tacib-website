04 — ABOUT TACIB

Independent by design.
Accountable by principle.

TACIB stands for The Agency of Cyber Intelligence Bangladesh. It is an independent private initiative working at the intersection of cybersecurity intelligence, defensive security, and public education.

TACIB is not a government agency, law-enforcement body, or regulator. Any service engagement is subject to a defined scope, appropriate authorization, confidentiality, and applicable law.
