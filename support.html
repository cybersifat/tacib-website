CYBER HELP & REPORTING

Find a safer next step. You don't have to guess.

TACIB shares general safety guidance and directs people toward relevant official reporting channels. We are not emergency responders, police, or a legal service, and we cannot promise investigations or content removal.

Immediate danger: if someone is in immediate physical danger, contact local emergency services or trusted in-person support. Do not wait for a reply from TACIB or an email inbox.

FIRST STEPS

Reduce harm while you seek help.

These are general safety steps. The right sequence can vary by platform, device, and whether the person may be monitoring your account.

01 / ACCOUNT ACCESS

Secure a trusted device

When safe, use a device you believe is not compromised. Change the affected account password and its recovery email password; enable MFA and revoke unknown sessions.

02 / EVIDENCE

Preserve relevant details

Save URLs, usernames, dates, messages, and report/reference numbers. Keep originals where safe. Do not edit evidence in a way that removes context.

03 / BLACKMAIL

Do not send more

Avoid sending additional money, intimate material, passwords, or verification codes. Consider support from a trusted person and report through the platform and suitable authorities.

04 / REPORTING

Choose a suitable route

Report directly to the affected platform and appropriate official service. TACIB cannot act as a substitute for a police report, court process, or emergency service.

BEFORE CONTACTING TACIB

Protect your privacy first.

  • Do not send passwords, recovery codes, private keys, full payment-card details, or government ID numbers.
  • Do not send intimate images or other sensitive personal content to TACIB.
  • Do not send large logs or files before a secure and appropriate transfer process has been agreed.
  • State the platform or system, a brief description, what help you are seeking, and whether you own or are authorized to manage the system.

TACIB may be unable to respond to every request. This page is informational and is not a monitored emergency hotline.
