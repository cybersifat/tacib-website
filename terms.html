TERMS OF USE

Use information responsibly.

These draft terms describe the intended use of the TACIB website and must be reviewed against applicable requirements before publication.

Last reviewed: 20 October 2026 · Draft for review; not legal advice.

Informational purpose

Website content is provided for general information and education. It is not a guarantee of security, legal advice, emergency response, or a substitute for guidance from a qualified professional appropriate to a specific situation.

Authorized use only

Any security testing discussed with TACIB requires documented authorization, a clear scope, and agreed rules before testing starts. Nothing on this website authorizes the visitor to test or access a third-party system.

No guaranteed outcome

Cybersecurity is risk reduction, not a guarantee that a system or account cannot be compromised. TACIB must define the work, limitations, deliverables, timing, and any fee in a separate written agreement before a paid engagement begins.

External information

External sites are provided for convenience and are not controlled by TACIB. Verify current instructions and the suitability of a reporting channel before submitting information.

Prohibited conduct

Do not use this site or contact channels to threaten, harass, dox, seek unauthorized access, submit malware, demand access to third-party information, or request unlawful activity.

Changes and contact

These terms are a draft and may be updated to reflect actual operations. Do not publish them as final legal terms before appropriate review.
