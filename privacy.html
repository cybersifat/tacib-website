PRIVACY NOTICE

Privacy should be part of the design.

This starter website is designed to minimize the personal information collected through its pages. Review and update this notice to match the services, hosting configuration, and actual practices before launch.

Last reviewed: 20 October 2026 · This is a practical draft for website configuration, not legal advice.

Information collected by this website

The static website does not include a server-side inquiry database, account registration, payment processing, or analytics integration by default. The inquiry builder runs in your browser and opens your local email application; it does not send the form directly to TACIB or save it in a website database.

The web-hosting and network providers may process technical information (such as request metadata and security logs) to deliver, protect, and maintain the service. Review the current provider terms and settings rather than assuming that no technical logging occurs.

Information you email to TACIB

If you choose to send an email, the email address, message, attachments, and metadata will be processed by the email services involved and by the recipient. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, private keys, intimate images, or unnecessary sensitive information. For a security engagement, agree on an appropriate data-transfer and retention method before sending sensitive files.

Cookies and analytics

This starter does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies. Browser storage or cookies may be used by hosting or external services if those services are added later. Reassess this statement whenever integrations change.

External links

Links to official reporting resources and other websites take you to independently operated services. Their privacy and security practices are governed by their own policies.

Retention, security, and requests

Email communications, if received, should be kept only as long as required for their purpose, applicable obligations, or legitimate recordkeeping, and protected with appropriate access controls. Publish a monitored privacy contact address before making this page final. Until then, use the contact page only after verifying the inbox shown there is active.

Changes

Update the review date and the contents of this notice whenever data handling changes. Do not claim legal compliance or make guarantees without reviewing the actual practices.
